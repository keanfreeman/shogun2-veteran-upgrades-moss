# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)
First version for Master of Strategy Sengoku, based on Veteran Upgrades 1.1.0.
- Unit types, recruitment and clan permissions generated from MoSS's data; MoSS's own units are
  covered (units without a multiplayer tree borrow one of the same class).
- One "(Veteran)" copy per unit with all of its abilities, bought as a single Abilities upgrade.
- Sengoku Jidai only.
